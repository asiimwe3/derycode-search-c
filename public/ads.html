How DeryCode Ads Work

1. Create a campaign with your ad title, description, and URL

2. Choose keywords you want to target (e.g. "website design Uganda")

3. Set your bid (how much you pay per click) and monthly budget

4. When someone searches your keywords on DeryCode Search, your ad appears at the top with a "Sponsored" label

5. You only pay when someone clicks your ad. No impression charges.

6. Track performance in real-time with click-through rates, spending, and budget usage.
