DeryCode AI Search Engine — Privacy-First Web Search

DeryCode AI is a free, private search engine that aggregates results from 23 sources including DuckDuckGo, Google, Wikipedia, Google Books, Google News, Google Scholar, PubMed, Project Gutenberg, Wikidata, CORE Research, World Bank Data, CrossRef, and Internet Archive. It offers 10 search modes: AI Answers, Web, Images, News, Code, Derick AI Assistant, Books, Maps, Deep Web, and Videos. The engine features AI-powered answer generation, knowledge panels, autocomplete, voice search, and multi-language support (English, Swahili, Luganda, Runyankole, Luo, Ateso). No tracking, no ads, no cookies. Built by DeryCode Technologies in Kampala, Uganda.
